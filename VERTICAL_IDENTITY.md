# Tenant Reply

Tenant Reply is the MailMyPDF vertical for tenant-side correspondence with landlords and property managers, including notices, lease issues, repair and habitability matters, eviction-related responses, and security-deposit disputes.

It is a distinct product domain from Notice Respond. Shared workflow infrastructure may be inherited from the MailMyPDF ecosystem, but workflow ownership belongs to Tenant Reply when the underlying problem is a tenant/property-management matter.

Canonical repository: `tenant-reply`.
